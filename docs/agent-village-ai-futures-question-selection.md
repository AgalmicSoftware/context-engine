# Agent Village Wrapped — AI Futures Question Selection

Condensed set for choosing AI futures questions in `agent-village-wrapped`.
These are not only about personal agents. The goal is to capture broader views
on AI futures, governance, society, work, culture, science, security, and human
meaning, while still being answerable by a personal agent predicting its
principal.

Use a smaller subset for launch; this document is meant to make selection easy.

## Questions To Avoid Or Reword

- "Do you think AI is good?" — too broad and socially scripted.
- "When will AGI arrive?" — interesting, but more forecasting than preference;
  use only if the session explicitly wants timelines.
- "Which AI model is best?" — brand loyalty, not durable futures data.
- "Should AI be regulated?" — too broad; ask about publication, compute,
  deployment, or liability instead.
- "Would you trust AI with important decisions?" — too vague; specify domain,
  review level, and reversibility.
- "Will AI kill everyone?" — too memetic/polarizing for this mixed research and
  Wrapped format; use narrower risk questions instead.

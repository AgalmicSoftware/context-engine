# Research data in interviews

When your own AI drafts your answers, a session can measure how well it knows
you: the agent mirror evaluation. Nothing is kept for it unless **Share for
research** is ticked when you submit.

![How the measure works: your AI drafts your answers from what it already knows about you. Shown drafts you keep or change. If a session hides some drafts, you answer those first, then see its guess. Each answer is compared with the guess: a match scores 1, and close ratings, votes and multiple choices score part. The result is the Mirror Score, how well your AI predicts you.](assets/research-data/how-the-measure-works.svg)

## What is kept

With each answer: your AI's guess, how sure it was, and what you changed. For
a hidden draft, also your answer before you saw the guess. If you allow it,
which AI and model you used. A few sessions also keep the AI's drafted text;
**What’s this?** beside **Share for research** says when.

## Who sees it

Only the session's admins, and you for your own answers.

## How to stop

Untick **Share for research** before you submit. To withdraw later, submit
again with it unticked; earlier saved versions and downloads aren't erased.

More detail: [how the score works](agent-fidelity.md) and
